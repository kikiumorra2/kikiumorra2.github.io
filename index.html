About

Hello, I am a research assistant at the Caplab at New York University.

I graduated from NYU in May, 2026 with a BA in Linguistics and Mathematics.

My research interests lie at the intersection of computational linguistics, psycholinguistics, and cognitive science. I am particularly interested in comparing model-derived representations with theories of syntax and sentence processing, as well as understanding how linguistic representations generalize across languages and in bilingual processing.
